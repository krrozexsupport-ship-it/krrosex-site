User Privacy Rights

Request Account & Data Deletion

Google Play Compliant Deletion Portal • Permanent Data Erasure within 7 Days

At Rose-X, we respect your fundamental right to control your personal data. In full compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, the GDPR, and international data protection standards, you may permanently delete your user account and all associated personal information at any time.

Two Ways to Delete Your Account

📱

Method 1: In-App Deletion

Open the Rose-X app > Navigate to Settings > Select Delete Account > Choose a reason > Tap Submit Request.

🌐

Method 2: Web Deletion Form

Complete the verified web form below, or send an email to krrozex.support@gmail.com without needing the app installed.

Web Deletion Request Form

Please provide the Google email address and/or User ID (UID) associated with your Rose-X account. Our legal & privacy team will verify and initiate your request immediately.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

  • Permanently Deleted: Your Google Account linkage, display name, email address, profile picture URL, academic quiz scores, vocabulary milestones, and unredeemed Roze Coin balances are permanently removed from our active databases within seven (7) business days.
  • Data Retention Exceptions: Completed transaction receipts, reward issuance logs, or records mandated by applicable anti-fraud and tax laws are archived securely in an anonymized format for the minimum statutory period.
  • Irreversible Action: Once deletion is finalized, this action cannot be undone. You will forfeit any unredeemed coin balance and will need to start fresh should you register again in the future.
