Privacy Policy

Welcome to KRRoseX (also referred to as "Rose X", "we", "our", or "us"). We are deeply committed to respecting your privacy, protecting your personal data, and maintaining complete transparency about how our educational platform operates. This Privacy Policy details the policies, mechanisms, and legal frameworks governing data collection, usage, disclosure, cross-border transfer, storage, and permanent deletion when you interact with the KRRoseX mobile application, services, and associated web properties (collectively, the "Services").

By downloading, accessing, installing, registering, or using KRRoseX, you expressly acknowledge that you have read, understood, and consented to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy in its entirety, you must discontinue using our Services.

1. Information We Collect and Process

We adhere strictly to the principle of Data Minimization. We collect and process only the minimal information strictly necessary to provide interactive English learning modules, manage gamified virtual coin rewards, fulfill verified payouts, ensure platform security, and prevent fraudulent abuse.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Authentication Information: When you register or sign in via Google Authentication (Firebase Auth), we receive your verified Google Account Name, Email Address, Profile Picture URL, and a unique numeric User Identifier (UID).
  • Profile & Regional Preferences: Your selected country code, preferred username, and chosen 3D Pixar avatar profile (e.g., indices `p1` through `p11`), used to display local currency payout options and personalize your leaderboard standing.
  • Payout & Redemption Data: When requesting reward redemption, you provide destination information:
    • India: Valid UPI ID (e.g., username@bank, PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm handle) or name for Flipkart digital vouchers.
    • International / Dollar Countries: Verified recipient email address and recipient name for delivery of Google Play, Amazon, Apple, or Starbucks digital claim codes.
  • Direct Communications: Content of feedback, bug reports, deletion requests, or customer support tickets sent to our official desk at support@krrosex.com.

B. Information Automatically Collected Through App Usage

  • Educational & Gamification Activity: Quiz results, vocabulary drills completed, pronunciation accuracy scores, daily 4-hour streak active time (tracked down to the millisecond: 14,400,000 ms), current streak day count, and your Roze Coins ledger balance.
  • Device & Diagnostic Telemetry: Device manufacturer, hardware model, Android operating system version, app version code, crash logs, and performance metrics collected via Google Firebase to troubleshoot bugs and guarantee stability.
  • Security & Anti-Fraud Signals: Timestamp logs, non-identifiable IP headers, and referral submission chains used solely to detect automated bot activity, emulators, auto-clickers, VPN spoofing, and duplicate multi-accounting.

C. Information We STRICTLY DO NOT Collect

  • We DO NOT collect or store credit card numbers, debit card PINs, CVVs, or net banking passwords.
  • We DO NOT read or access your SMS text messages, call logs, contacts, camera roll, or personal files.
  • We DO NOT track or collect fine real-time GPS coordinates.
  • We DO NOT store raw biometric facial or voiceprint identifiers. Audio from speaking exercises is evaluated client-side or streamed transiently solely for pronunciation feedback and is never stored or monetized.

2. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

For users in the European Union, European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and jurisdictions requiring explicit legal grounds, we process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: Delivering your English educational lessons, tracking quiz scores, managing your Roze Coin balance, and delivering requested payout rewards pursuant to our Terms & Conditions.
  • Legitimate Interests: Protecting our community against automated scripts, emulator exploitation, and financial fraud; optimizing application performance; and improving our educational curriculum.
  • Legal Obligation: Complying with statutory financial reporting, anti-money laundering frameworks, tax records, and legitimate law enforcement orders.
  • Consent: Where required (e.g., optional push notifications or joining our official Telegram channel), which you may revoke at any time without penalty.

3. Third-Party Services & Developer Policy Disclosures

We DO NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers, advertising agencies, or marketing networks. We share data only with trusted technical service providers subject to strict confidentiality agreements and Data Processing Addendums (DPAs):

  • Google LLC (Firebase): Used for secure user authentication (Firebase Auth), real-time database storage (Firebase Realtime Database and Cloud Firestore), and crash analytics. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Core Android system services for authentication tokens and application integrity verification.
  • Merchant Voucher Providers: Digital claim codes and UPI payment gateways receive only the necessary transaction identifiers (UPI ID or email) solely to deliver the reward selected by the user.

4. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion Policy

We retain your personal data only for as long as your account remains active or as required to deliver our educational and reward services.

  • Absolute Right to Deletion: You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated personal information at any time, in full compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy.
  • In-App Deletion: Open KRRoseX > Navigate to Settings > Select "Delete Account" > Choose a reason > Confirm submission.
  • Web Deletion Portal: You may submit an instant erasure request anytime through our Account Deletion Web Portal without having the app installed on your device.
  • 7-Day Permanent Erasure Guarantee: Once a deletion request is submitted, all personal identifiers, email, display name, avatar index, quiz history, and coin ledger entries are permanently and irreversibly purged from our active databases within seven (7) business days.

5. Country-Specific Privacy Rights & Legal Compliance

We provide full, robust, and equal privacy protections to all learners worldwide, respecting the statutory laws of each supported nation:

A. India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 & IT Act 2000)

In accordance with the DPDP Act 2023 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011:

  • Personal data is processed strictly for the specified purpose to which the user has consented.
  • Users possess the statutory right to access a summary of their processed personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, request erasure, and register grievances.
  • Statutory Grievance Officer: Inquiries, disputes, and compliance notices may be directed to our designated Grievance Officer at support@krrosex.com. Grievances are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within statutory timeframes.

B. European Union & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation 2016/679) and UK Data Protection Act 2018, European and British users enjoy comprehensive rights:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Request confirmation and an electronic copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Demand correction of incomplete or incorrect data.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17): Request irrevocable deletion of all your data.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18): Restrict processing under specified legal grounds.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your personal data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Cross-Border Transfers: When transferring personal data outside the EEA or UK, we rely on European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to ensure equivalent protection.

C. United States (California CCPA / CPRA & State Privacy Laws)

For residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and other US states with consumer privacy statutes:

  • Categories of Personal Information Collected: Identifiers (name, email, Google UID, IP address), commercial history (reward redemptions), and internet activity (quiz performance).
  • No Sale or Sharing: We DO NOT sell personal information and DO NOT share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not sold or shared user data in the preceding 12 months.
  • Notice of Financial Incentive: Earning Roze Coins and redeeming rewards is a bona fide loyalty/gamification program designed to incentivize English language learning. Participation is 100% voluntary and requires no purchase.
  • Non-Discrimination: You will not face discrimination, penalty, or service denial for exercising your CCPA rights.

D. Canada (PIPEDA & Provincial Privacy Acts)

We adhere to the 10 Fair Information Principles of the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Quebec Law 25. Canadian users have the right to access and challenge the accuracy of their data and withdraw consent at any time.

E. Australia (Privacy Act 1988 & Australian Privacy Principles - APPs)

We comply with the 13 Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Australian learners may inspect their data, correct inaccuracies, or lodge formal complaints directly with our team or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

F. Japan (Act on the Protection of Personal Information - APPI)

Japanese users' data is processed in strict compliance with the APPI. You have the right to request disclosure of retained personal data, correction, deletion, and cessation of third-party provision.

G. South Korea (Personal Information Protection Act - PIPA)

In accordance with South Korea's PIPA, user data is immediately destroyed upon attaining the specified educational purpose or upon account termination. Overseas server transfers to Google Firebase cloud servers are conducted strictly to provide our core services and secured with end-to-end encryption.

H. Singapore (Personal Data Protection Act 2012 - PDPA)

We comply with Singapore's PDPA regarding consent, purpose limitation, notification, accuracy, protection, and retention obligations.

I. United Arab Emirates (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection)

In full accordance with the UAE PDPL, personal data is processed lawfully, transparently, and securely, with comprehensive rights granted to UAE data subjects regarding access and erasure.

J. Switzerland (Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection - revFADP)

We adhere to the revised Swiss FADP guaranteeing robust cross-border data protection, rapid user disclosure, and right of erasure.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

KRRoseX is an educational application intended for general audiences aged 13 years and older (or 16 years and older where local jurisdiction mandates a higher digital consent age, such as the EU). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal data from children under 13.

If we discover that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we will immediately take swift steps to permanently purge such data from our servers. Parents or guardians may contact us at support@krrosex.com for immediate removal.

7. Technical Data Security Safeguards

We implement rigorous technical, organizational, and physical security measures to protect your information from loss, theft, unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • All network communications between the mobile application and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Database access is locked behind granular Firebase Security Rules, enforcing user-token verification so that no user can view or modify another user's personal data.
  • Administrative access to payout logs is protected by hardware-backed multi-factor authentication and continuous audit trails.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new educational features, or evolving legal standards. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. For significant changes, we will provide prominent in-app notification prior to implementation. Continued use of KRRoseX after updates indicates your acknowledgment and acceptance of the revised policy.
