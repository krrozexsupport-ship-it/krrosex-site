Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026 • Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Google Play Compliant GDPR (EU & UK) CCPA / CPRA (US) DPDP Act (India) PIPEDA (Canada) Privacy Act (Australia) APPI (Japan) PIPA (South Korea)

Welcome to KRRoseX (also referred to as "Rose X", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles, practices, and procedures regarding the collection, storage, processing, transfer, and deletion of information when you access or use our mobile application, services, and associated website (collectively, the "Services").

By downloading, accessing, or using KRRoseX, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and consented to the data collection and processing practices described herein. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our Services.

1. Information We Collect

We strictly follow the principle of data minimization, collecting only information necessary to deliver our educational language learning modules, manage gamified reward balances, verify legitimate payout redemptions, and prevent fraudulent abuse.

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Authentication Information: When you register or sign in via Google Authentication (Firebase Auth), we receive your Google Account Name, Email Address, Google Profile Photo URL, and a unique numeric User Identifier (UID).
  • Country & Profile Preferences: Your selected country code, preferred name, and selected 3D avatar index used to personalize your learning environment and display local reward currency.
  • Reward & Payout Information: When you submit a withdrawal request, you provide destination details such as your UPI ID (for India) or your verified Email Address and Recipient Name (for Google Play, Amazon, Apple, and Starbucks gift cards).
  • Support Communications: Content of communications, feedback, bug reports, and deletion inquiries sent to our support team.

B. Information Automatically Collected

  • Academic & Learning Activity: Your grammar quiz progress, vocabulary milestones, speaking exercise accuracy scores, daily 4-hour goal active usage timestamps, streak status, and accumulated Roze Coins.
  • Microphone & Audio Processing (Speaking Practice): When you participate in interactive English speaking practice, the app requests runtime permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) solely to capture voice input for real-time speech-to-text evaluation via system SpeechRecognizer. We DO NOT record, store, or transmit your raw voice audio to our servers. All voice processing is handled transiently in real time to assess pronunciation accuracy.
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: Operating system version (Android), device model, application version code, crash logs, and performance metrics collected via Firebase to maintain app stability.
  • Advertising Telemetry (Future AdMob Integration): Once live on the Google Play Store, Google AdMob may collect the Google Advertising ID (GAID) and ad interaction metrics to deliver compliant contextual or consented personalized advertisements.
  • Anti-Fraud Signals: Timestamp logs, non-identifiable network telemetry, and referral submission logs to detect automated bot activity, emulators, duplicate registrations, and abusive exploitation of our coin rewards.

C. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • We DO NOT collect or store credit card numbers, debit card PINs, bank account passwords, or CVVs.
  • We DO NOT record, store, or upload your personal voice audio files, microphone recordings, or phone call audio to our servers.
  • We DO NOT access your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, camera, or personal media files.
  • We DO NOT track or collect precise real-time GPS coordinates.
  • We DO NOT collect biometric identifiers (fingerprints, facial recognition data).

2. Legal Basis for Data Processing

Depending on your jurisdiction, we process your personal data under the following lawful grounds:

  • Performance of Contract: To provide our interactive language quizzes, record your educational progress, compute virtual coin balances, and deliver requested gift card or transfer rewards.
  • Legitimate Interests: To protect our platform from automated script exploitation, verify genuine quiz participation, troubleshoot crashes, and optimize educational content.
  • Legal Compliance: To adhere to applicable statutory requirements, tax regulations, anti-fraud standards, and respond to lawful government requests.
  • Consent: For optional marketing updates, Telegram community access, or optional feature participation, which you may withdraw at any time.

3. Third-Party Services & Disclosures

We DO NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information to data brokers, advertising aggregators, or third parties for their own commercial marketing purposes. We work exclusively with trusted service providers bound by strict confidentiality and data protection obligations:

  • Google LLC (Firebase): Used for secure user authentication (Firebase Auth), cloud database synchronization (Firebase Realtime Database and Cloud Firestore), and crash diagnostics. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services & Speech Services: Core Android libraries enabling authentication, application integrity verification, and transient real-time speech recognition for interactive language exercises without persistent audio storage.
  • Google AdMob (Advertising Partner - Post-Launch Integration): Please note that at initial launch, KRRoseX does not display commercial advertisements. However, following official availability on the Google Play Store, we plan to integrate Google AdMob (Google LLC) to serve contextual, non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads to support free educational access. When enabled, Google AdMob processes information including your Google Advertising ID (GAID), device brand/model, IP address, and ad interaction metrics in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable global data protection regulations (such as GDPR consent mechanisms and CCPA opt-out choices). Users retain full authority to reset their Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized ads at any time via Android Device Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Reward & Payout Delivery (24-Hour Fulfillment): Digital redemption codes (Amazon, Apple, Google Play, Starbucks, Flipkart) and direct UPI routing networks receive only essential destination details (UPI ID or verified recipient email address) solely to execute payouts. All legitimate, verified withdrawal requests are processed and fulfilled within 24 hours.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

We retain your personal information only for as long as your account is active, or as necessary to fulfill the educational and reward purposes described in this policy.

  • 7-Day Deletion Guarantee: You possess the absolute right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.
  • In-App Deletion: Open KRRoseX > Settings > Delete Account > Submit Request.
  • Web-Based Deletion: You may submit an online erasure request directly at our Account Deletion Web Portal without requiring the app to be installed.
  • Erasure Process: Upon receiving your request, our systems irrevocably purge your personal details, email, name, profile picture, and learning records from our active databases within seven (7) business days. Non-identifiable aggregated statistics or completed transaction receipts required by law are archived securely.

5. International & Country-Specific Privacy Rights

We provide full, equal, and comprehensive privacy protections to our users globally in strict accordance with the laws of each country supported by KRRoseX:

A. European Economic Area (EEA) & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you reside within the European Union, European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, you are entitled to the following rights under Articles 15–22 of the GDPR:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Obtain confirmation of whether your personal data is being processed and receive a portable copy.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal details.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17): Demand permanent deletion of your data where no overriding legal requirement exists.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18): Restrict processing under specified legal grounds.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct communications.
  • Supervisory Authority: You have the right to lodge a formal complaint with your national Data Protection Authority (DPA) or the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

B. United States (California CCPA / CPRA & State Privacy Laws)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents enjoy specific rights:

  • Notice at Collection: We disclose the categories of personal data collected, purposes, and retention timelines.
  • Right to Know & Access: You may request disclosure of categories of personal information collected, sources, business purpose, and third parties with whom data is shared.
  • Right to Delete & Correct: Request deletion or correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • No Sale or Sharing of Personal Information: We do NOT sell your personal information and do NOT share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding 12 months.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, charge different prices, or provide a lesser quality of service should you exercise your privacy rights.

C. India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 & IT Act 2000)

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) and Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011:

  • Personal data is processed strictly for the verified purpose consented to by the user.
  • Users have the right to access a summary of data processed, request correction or erasure, and nominate another individual in the event of death or incapacity.
  • We maintain comprehensive administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard user information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.
  • Grievance Redressal: Inquiries or grievances can be addressed to our designated Grievance Officer at krrozex.support@gmail.com, with resolution provided within 30 days.

D. Canada (PIPEDA & Provincial Privacy Acts)

Under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Canadian residents have the right to access and challenge the accuracy and completeness of their personal information held by us, and to withdraw consent at any time subject to legal restrictions.

E. Australia (Privacy Act 1988 & Australian Privacy Principles - APPs)

We adhere to the 13 Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Australian users may request access to personal data, correct inaccurate details, and lodge privacy complaints directly with our team or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

F. Japan (Act on the Protection of Personal Information - APPI)

We process personal data of Japanese users in compliance with the APPI. Users can request notification of the purpose of use, disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, or cessation of provision to third parties of retained personal data.

G. South Korea (Personal Information Protection Act - PIPA)

In accordance with South Korea's PIPA, user information is destroyed without delay when the purpose of collection is attained or upon user request. Overseas data transfers to Google Firebase cloud servers are conducted solely for core service provision and secured with end-to-end encryption.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

KRRoseX is an educational application intended for general audiences aged 13 years and older (or 16 years and older in the European Economic Area / UK). We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children under the age of 13.

If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information without verified parental consent, we will immediately take steps to permanently delete such information from our servers. Parents or legal guardians who believe their child has submitted personal details may contact us at krrozex.support@gmail.com for prompt removal.

7. Data Security Measures

We employ industry-standard technical, organizational, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information from accidental loss, unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. All data transmissions between the application and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Database access is restricted to authorized systems through strict Firebase Security Rules, token-based verification, and multi-factor authentication.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational modifications, feature enhancements, or statutory legal updates. When changes are made, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will provide prominent notification inside the mobile application or via email prior to the changes taking effect. Your continued use of the Services after the effective date constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
