Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding coin mechanics, payout verifications, eligibility, and data privacy.

Is KRRoseX 100% free to use? +
Yes! KRRoseX is completely free to download and use. We never request bank card details, deposits, registration fees, or mandatory paid subscriptions. All learning modules and reward redemptions are funded purely by our educational partners and platform sponsors.
How do I claim my 100 welcome bonus coins? +
When you launch the app and sign in with Google for the first time, a "First Login Reward" modal automatically appears. Simply tap "Claim 100 Coins" to have them credited to your wallet instantly!
How does the 4-Hour Daily Streak goal work? +
To build genuine language proficiency, our habit tracking engine measures your cumulative daily active screen time (14,400,000 milliseconds). If you remain idle for more than 90 seconds, tracking pauses automatically so only focused practice counts towards your streak fire 🔥.
How long does it take to receive payouts? +
To prevent fraud and maintain platform fairness, redemption requests are audited and verified within 24 to 48 business hours. The moment your payout is marked successful in our system, you will receive an in-app celebration popup and your code or UPI transfer is delivered immediately.
Can I use VPNs, emulators, or multiple accounts? +
No. To protect genuine learners, our automated security engine strictly prohibits Android emulators (BlueStacks, Nox), macros, bots, cloned accounts, and VPN location spoofing. Violators face permanent account suspension and coin forfeiture under our Terms and Conditions.
Which countries and currencies are supported? +
KRRoseX is an international platform! Dedicated payout currencies and localized cards are active for India (INR), the United States, Canada, and Australia (USD/CAD/AUD), the United Kingdom (GBP), European Union (EUR), Japan (JPY), South Korea (KRW), UAE (AED), Singapore (SGD), and Switzerland (CHF).
How can I delete my account and personal data? +
We adhere strictly to Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, GDPR, and DPDP Act 2023. You can request deletion directly in the app under Settings > Delete Account, or submit an online request via our Account Deletion Web Portal. All data is purged within 7 business days.
How does the referral bonus program work? +
Every learner receives a unique referral code. When a friend joins using your code and completes their first quiz, both you and your friend receive an instant 100-coin bonus in your wallets!
